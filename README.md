## Phone2Key — Android beta 5.7

**Phone2Key comes first:** Unlock, Lock and Trunk on Home, with separate Bluetooth-delivery and car-reported results. The app also includes cabin climate start/stop, recurring schedules and separate temperature, fan, A/C, Auto, recirculation, outlets and sync controls.

**Existing testers:** [open the Play beta link](https://play.google.com/apps/internaltest/4701636999134573001) using the same Google account as your phone's Play Store and update to **5.7 (build 12)**. **New testers:** [request access](https://wavestripe.app/account#android-beta), wait for acceptance, then choose Become a tester and install. The app may display a temporary package-name listing while the store review is unfinished.

Phone2Key requires Pro, a supported phone and the operator-provisioned credential for your own car. Installing the update does not automatically enrol a new car key. Key actions work within Bluetooth range, offline during a valid lease; initial setup and renewal need internet. Scheduled climate also requires Alarms & reminders and notifications, with the phone near the car at the scheduled time.
